Lidar-guided aiming (design draft, 2026-10-25)

Design · 2026-10-25 · sim and 2026-10-25 robot session evidence; replaces the Jetson head patrol

Stage 1 is built (no Jetson patrol, idle looks, fire age limit); stages 2-4 are open. Evidence: the 2026-10-25 robot session (Evidence). Game rules: ARCC context.

Why

Jetson patrol breaks rf2o because the lidar sits on the head.
The RPLIDAR is 0.22 m off the yaw axis (lidar_self_filter); patrol turns at -2 rad/s, about 0.19 rad per 93 ms sweep. rf2o's per-scan step grew from 0.7 mm (head still) to about 23 mm (patrol), and the parked reference check failed under patrol (82.9 mm steps, 0.81 m drift).
The lidar does not need head motion to cover the field; only the camera does.
It sees about 285 deg whichever way the head faces (blind only 6-79.5 deg CCW of the gun).

Firmware keeps the last frame's flags. If the Jetson goes silent after a target frame (which clears turn_to_hit), firmware hit turns stop working, so any no-patrol design must keep sending non-firing frames. On a hit, firmware turns the head toward the hit armor's side (util/HitOrientation.hpp) and reports it on REF_SYS.

Decided

  • No Jetson patrol.
  • Fire on a prediction only while the newest detection is at most 0.15 s old (fire_max_state_age_s); keep aiming without firing until target_timeout_s (0.5 s).
  • room_check stale-CV mode is a diagnostic, not a requirement; YOLO outages need no design beyond the stack restarting.
  • Shooting with no referee engages every robot (already true: team_from_ref_sys returns no team for robot_id 0).
  • Firmware hit handling unchanged; hold 2 s after a hit. Obstacles come from the saved field map.

Design

Where the head looks with no camera target

Non-firing frames, turn_to_hit set, patrol bit set (meaning: not a target point). In order:

  1. Last enemy, 3 s after losing it: follow the prediction until 0.5 s, then hold its last position.
  2. Hit, 2 s: follow the gun so the head stays where firmware's hit turn left it. The newer of 1 and 2 wins.
  3. Lidar track worth checking (head integration is stage 4).
  4. Enemy starting zone. Raw odometry is field-centred: firmware boots red at (-START_X, START_Y) facing +x, blue at (+START_X, START_Y) facing -x (OdometrySubsystemConstants.hpp; START_X 4.625 m, not yet measured on the field). The enemy zone is our start with x negated. Team comes from the REF_SYS blue flag as firmware uses it, so with no referee both sides assume red.

Aim height is armor height (~0.15 m; Standard-class panels sit 60-150 mm up), not lidar height. Turns are rate capped to limit lidar smear.

Lidar tracker (new lidar_tracker node, thornbots_pkg)

  • Uses /scan, skipping scans taken while the head turns fast.
  • Opponents taller than the 0.355 m scan plane mostly show their head: small clusters, but on the yaw axis, so the centre is steady while the chassis spins.
  • Size from our own head as stand-in (sentry_v2 CAD sliced at z 0.275-0.425 m, within 0.13 m of the yaw axis, excluding the lidar arm): 0.15-0.25 m wide, median 0.23 m. Accept clusters of roughly 0.10-0.40 m.
  • Obstacles from the saved map (/map, in localization_mode amcl and slam): scan points within ~0.25 m of an occupied cell (absorbing localization and URDF mount error) are obstacles; the rest may be robots. A robot that close to a wall is missed.
  • No map frame (none) or a live map (mapping, which can map robots): no lidar looks; the enemy-zone look still works.
  • Tracks hold position and velocity a few seconds through dropouts (tilt on the Bumpy Road or ramps moves the plane ~7 cm per degree at 4 m). Each is unchecked, enemy (camera confirmed) or checked-empty (teammate or false cluster; ignored for a while).
  • Ranking: enemy > unchecked, then moving/approaching, then closer. Look ~0.6 s; no enemy found marks it checked.
  • Tracks live in map. A look only has to land the robot in the camera's view (~1 m at 2-3 m), but obstacle removal needs localization within the margin: a misaligned map turns walls into candidates.

Hits

No firmware change. The Jetson keeps the hit turn permitted (turn_to_hit on every non-target frame) and, for 2 s after REF_SYS reports a hit, follows the gun instead of pulling the head back.

Stages

Stages 2–4 are paused (2026-11-05). Stage 1 merged. Stage 2 makes robots visible in sim lidar, stage 3 adds visualization-only tracker output with no head effect, stage 4 adds ranked-track head direction. These are interface boundaries; status, ordering, dependencies and acceptance live only in the roadmap. This design defines saved-map rejection, ranking, confirmation and head priorities.

Risks

  • Detection range is unmeasured. A 0.23 m head spans about 6-7 of the 0.5 deg bins at 4 m and about 3 at 8 m, before missing returns (~408 of 720 bins return per scan); other teams' heads may differ.
  • Head-sized clutter (posts, corners, ramp edges); robots on the High Ground or with heads off the plane; teammates cost one look each.
  • Turning toward targets still smears scans; rf2o/AMCL should skip fast-turn scans (localization side). The URDF lidar offset (~10 cm) shifts localization when the head turns until the new CAD lands.
  • Staring at the zone covers less than patrol did until stage 4; compare time-to-first-detection in the sim match stages before promoting.

Acceptance before main

Software gates: ROADMAP T23/A-model. Physical gates: ROBOT_TESTS T23.
