Leading cause (associational): variable real scan length breaks rf2o, and the EKF has no raw x/y anchor.
Real scans have 335–358 beams (archived 340–361) with angle_compensate=false; sim is a fixed 800. rf2o fixes width/FOV from the first scan (CLaserOdometry2D.cpp:69) and applies it to every later scan (:210), so a shorter scan is read past its valid vector and a longer one truncated. The EKF fuses raw yaw/vx/vy but not raw x/y, and rf2o x/y absolute (sentry_localization/config/ekf.yaml), so zero raw odom does not anchor position.
Scan-size normalization/guard is merged (rf2o #10).
The parked-reference follow-up is maintained in ROBOT_TESTS T39; the historical proposal is not a second backlog.
