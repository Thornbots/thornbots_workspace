Failures and limits

  • The first broad field circuit with rf2o/EKF SLAM input diverged beyond 5.4 m and was stopped. The second, raw-input circuit covered about 50 m but reached 10.23 m error. Neither map is deployable. Terrain tilt is a suspected contributor (the raw run crossed a raised region, truth z about 0.083 m, with chassis heading drift) but the experiment does not isolate the cause. Planar matching, terrain and odometry need further investigation.
  • The second run's alignment command hit argparse's negative scientific-notation parsing (--yaw -2e-05); the harness now uses --yaw=VALUE. It never advanced to AMCL on that bad map.
  • The initial room parked score failed 95% TF coverage (90.97%) because scoring began before discovery. The failed evidence is kept; the explicit readiness wait produced the reported repeat without removing samples.
  • Real hardware was not run. The Oct 18 investigation saw 340–361 beams with varying angular increments rejected by the localization stack. Current lidar config enables angle compensation, but its real stream is unvalidated. The workflow rejects inconsistent geometry clearly.
  • Wheel slip, surveyed heading, lidar extrinsics, dynamic obstacles and thin rasterized features remain hardware risks. Visual scan/wall inspection and adequate map coverage are mandatory; filter convergence is not truth.
  • Field registration affects localization only. Firmware START_X/Y, raw aim coordinates and routes remain the team's responsibility; the operator must supply current firmware odometry seeds separately from measured physical pose. No movement or firmware adaptation was attempted.
  • Saved graphs stay in their original boot frame. Transformed graph reload and the existing saved-graph zero-pose startup are not implemented or rehearsed; recovery is remapping, or documented odometry-only operation with no map-localization claim. No PR was merged and no main promotion was performed.
