Oct 25 head-turn displacement: offline evidence

Raw-scan registration reproduces a CAD discrepancy of about 160 mm.
Six settled scans (three near 0, three near +0.65 rad), all nine pairs with three yaw guesses, trimmed ICP (closest 80%, under 0.3 m, ranges up to 8 m): 27 fits, retained RMS 25.9–32.1 mm. Median effective u = Rz(-delta) l is (-0.202956, -0.104684) m versus CAD u (-0.075027, -0.206811) m, a 163.7 mm difference. An earlier selection gave (-0.2051, -0.1111) m, 161 mm. A 108-fit sweep over retained fractions 0.6–0.9 moves median u only from about (-0.199, -0.111) to (-0.210, -0.101) m. These dependent fits give sensitivity ranges, not confidence intervals. Limits: scene changes, partial overlap, limited heading separation, nearest-neighbour fitting.
The combined CAD translation and yaw is inconsistent with the fits; neither conditional extrinsic is a calibration.
CAD translation held fixed: best direction-only yaw +34.21°, 8.36 mm radius residual. CAD yaw held fixed: implied translation (-0.216287, -0.073280) m. (The measured fit above later gave 33.84°.)
Parked registration constrains u = Rz(-delta) l, not translation and yaw separately.
If scan B registers into A by p_A = R(theta) p_B + t, then t = (R(theta)-I) u. Absolute scan-zero yaw is not separately observable from parked rotations without an external heading/geometry constraint. The authored FOV yaw stays unverified here.
Original session CSV fits are diagnostic only.
The original fit implementation is unavailable and its convention cannot be certified from the rounded report. Refitting five CSV windows gives head-frame translation (-0.2007, -0.1169) m ignoring map yaw, (-0.1844, -0.1261) m with reported SLAM yaw. SLAM yaw ratchets from 0 to 0.0942 rad after the positive turn and ends near 0.1082 rad; a position-only fit cannot explain that. Positive/negative-turn estimates disagree by centimetres. The first recorded TF yaw was about +0.1036 rad but the baseline before the deliberate turns was about zero. Raw scans (step4/bag/bag_0.mcap) are from later Oct 25, not the ±90° CSV run; recorded pod displacement between those headings is about 1 mm.
What is established and what is not.
CAD-to-head Rz(+90°) is a proper rotation, and converting the CAD -z yaw joint to ROS +z reverses its coordinate. Raw scans register at the recorded positive head rotation with small residual yaw, so a wholesale reversed yaw sign is poorly supported. Swapping old negative x/y gives a vector near the fits, but a swap alone is a reflection and numerical proximity does not establish that failure. A wrong yaw-axis location could alter the lever arm; a fixed root-position offset cancels. The old head-joint XY offset is 0.196 mm and cannot explain this.
