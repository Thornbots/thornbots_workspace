Operator and hardware defaults differ.
Operator mapping/localize default to raw passthrough; hardware auto.launch.py defaults to mapping with rf2o/EKF.
Raw boot has no referee/start gate, and EKF seed flags do nothing with raw passthrough.
Standalone convergence after reboot supplies the parked team/raw-start, scan/TF and covariance checks. Blue registration uses pi but AMCL root yaw is zero for both teams; deployment uses deployment/field without extension.
Manual activation does not match the service environment.
Service stop restarts timesyncd, while manual activation neither stops NTP nor installs the clock watcher: synchronize before ROS and freeze NTP. Missing overlay setup silently falls back to baked packages. Hardware development launch defaults to /dev/ttyUSB0 (verify the lidar); the service passes /host-dev/rplidar.
