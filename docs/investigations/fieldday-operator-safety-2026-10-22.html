Changes and checks

The workflow no longer takes hand-entered seeds and checks the session end to end.
sentry_localization e8e80cd: waits for fresh raw odometry before EKF startup, validates team/sign/heading against boot/referee evidence, ties boot, save and registration to one session ID. Explicit saves require returning within 0.15 m / 0.15 rad of boot; autosaves use a separate backup directory; unsaved stops warn. Localization runs parked-convergence checks and prints a final verdict. The yaw guard rejects degrees but accepts negative scientific notation and float32 noise at pi. thornbots_pkg 71a5a19 forwards safe seeding and backup paths and clarifies the AMCL y default. No tuning, firmware, route, movement or odometry-input change.

Failed attempts and limits

  • A stationary-only red map left the start cell unknown; alignment correctly refused it. A small mapping circuit fixed that; the occupancy gate was not weakened.
  • An intermediate harness applied parked accuracy gates to the deliberately offset, unregistered mapping frame. Mapping metrics are diagnostic; only the registered AMCL runs claim accuracy.
  • Blue alignment exposed float32 pi just above double pi. The guard now allows 1e-6 rad serialization noise (with a regression test) and still rejects degrees.
  • Not covered: real hardware, lidar stream consistency, surveyed extrinsics, slip, terrain, full-field coverage, broad-field SLAM divergence. No physical-robot runs or autonomous movement/firmware changes.
