Parked rf2o drift (T39)

Investigation · 2026-10-25 · sim only, hardware untested

Historical evidence. Live commitments: software/sim roadmap, physical validation. Workspace #95 ships reference_scans off by default; the runs below are opt-in experiments, not validation of that default.

Finding

A parked robot's navigation pose wandered because rf2o integrates scan-to-scan match noise and the EKF takes rf2o x/y as its only position source. rf2o's reference_scans matches a parked scan against an earlier scan from the same head angle, so the error stops integrating. Mechanism and parameters: rf2o README; tuning: sentry_localization.

Claims and evidence

Drift comes from rf2o integration, not head motion or wheels.
Wheel odometry held within 2.3 mm (chart). Nine earlier runs drifted 0.54 mm/s with the head still and 0.62–0.65 mm/s slewing (A/B, 2026-10-23). AMCL never reaches update_min_d while parked, so nothing bounds it.
References cut parked error about tenfold.
mcb_parked p95, 5 runs per arm, all 12 cells pass: off 0.005–0.090 m, on 0.002–0.009 m. Hit rate unchanged (0.896 vs 0.907).
Driving does not regress.
References are used only while /odom is within 5 mm for 2 s, so driving runs the unchanged scan-to-scan path. mcb_drive 3/3, EKF 8/8, AMCL drift 9/9. Earlier designs that kept references while moving raised AMCL corrections and were dropped.
A shove still moves the estimate.
parked_push (wheels held, truth 41 mm): estimate within 4–6 mm on, 2.5–11 mm off.
Combined with startup recovery (rf2o #16) it still passes.
47/47 rf2o tests, AMCL drift 3/3, mcb_parked 3/3 (worst all-cell p95 11 mm), EKF 5/5. Drive 5/6: one unexplained route-completion failure is counted, not dismissed.

Runs: Mac mini, own container, nightly 7b17b54 pins. Raw logs and bags are local to the Mac mini (~/ros2_ws/t39-parked-drift-20261025/, ~/ros2_ws/rf2o-combined-20261025/) and not published.

Limits

  • Sim lidar noise is constant 1 cm Gaussian; real RPLIDAR noise, dropouts and moving obstacles may change how often references drop.
  • A robot creeping more than 5 mm in 2 s falls back to scan-to-scan.
  • A parked shove reads up to 11 mm short with or without references: rf2o blends each match with the still wheel prior.
