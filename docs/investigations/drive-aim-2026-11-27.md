# mcb_match drive-phase aim error (2026-11-27)

We measured where the Sentry's drive-phase shots miss in hosted `mcb_match`,
after making same-seed runs repeatable. Changes:
[thornbots_pkg #87](https://github.com/Thornbots/thornbots_pkg/pull/87),
[sim #130](https://github.com/Thornbots/sim/pull/130),
[sim #131](https://github.com/Thornbots/sim/pull/131).

## Repeatable runs

Two same-seed runs split in the drive leg. `target_manager`'s 5 ms timer
decided confidence-decay clears at whatever time its callback ran. It also
decided estimates at its own `now()`, which can trail the tracker's clock. A
clear at 18.793 s came in one run and not in the other (first differing
`MCB_UART_TRACE` frame). The fixes:

- Ticks run on a clock grid. A late callback catches up on the ticks it
  missed, an estimate first runs the ticks due by its own time, and no
  estimate is decided before its stamp.
- Sim holds lockstep on each tick.
- The MCB plugin's 1.5 s wall guard waits 60 s, like the coordinator's. It
  had failed bring-up at 2 CPUs.

## Baseline: 15 distinct encounters

The encounters used `red_delay_s` 0–1.5 s, seeds 1–16 and `detector_noise:=true`.
One of the 16 runs hit the bring-up `nav_goal` ack timeout. The build is
workspace #252.

| Segment | Valid / attempts | Hits/s | Median miss | RMS right: miss / tracker / prediction / aim |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Drive | 78/141 (55.3%) | 3.58 | 5.6 cm | 8.7 / 3.9 / 2.7 / 6.1 cm |
| Parked | 89/96 (92.7%) | 5.03 | 2.5 cm | 5.6 / 2.8 / 1.2 / 5.1 cm |
| Spin | 421/491 (85.7%) | 2.66 | 3.4 cm | 2.3 / 0.9 / 0.6 / 1.8 cm |

Spin is 85.7% here. It is 99–100% without detector noise, and its vertical
tracker error is 4.3 cm RMS. Without noise, seed 1 with red delays 0 and
0.4 s (four runs) gives drive 16/37, parked 25/25 and spin 118/118.

## What limits drive

The firmware logged its own predicted impact for each accepted fire request
(3 encounters, 30 drive shots, hosted debug build). It matches the scorer's
aim part with correlation 0.77 and an RMS difference of 3.1 cm.

- **Edge firing while the head lags.** The full-outline gate allows a
  shot anywhere in the 13.5 × 12.5 cm plate. In 7 of 9 still-target misses,
  the firmware already predicted the impact more than 3 cm off centre. While
  the Sentry drives, the plan moves at ~1 rad/s and the head trails it by
  0.02–0.08 rad. The first shot of each drive fires while the head still
  slews (head rate 1.3–1.9 rad/s against a plan rate of 0.3–0.4 rad/s).
- **Tracker and prediction bias in the same direction.** On those misses
  the tracker part averages −2.9 cm and the prediction part −1.8 cm right,
  the same side as the head's lag. The tracked centre's velocity is
  0.2–0.3 m/s off across the line of sight.
- **Spin-up lag.** About a third of drive misses come while the ghost spins
  up at 20 rad/s². The lead is the w=0-prior moving filter throughout; the
  still hypothesis is not involved. Its w trails truth by 2–4 rad/s, and the
  tracker part reaches 6–14 cm.

## Variants tried (not shipped)

Paired with the baseline on the same encounters:

| Variant | Encounters | Drive | Parked | Spin |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| `process_noise_yaw_accel` 15 | 7 | 54.3% → 53.9%, aim RMS 5.6 → 6.6 cm | 100% → 100% | 86.9% → 85.4% |
| `single_panel_yaw_std` 0.6 | 7 | 54.3% → 49.3% | 100% → 97.9% | 86.9% → 88.9% |
| Facing as a ±0.26 rad band, not a pin | 14 | 55.8% → 51.2% | 92.3% → 88.0% | 85.6% → 91.0% |
| Gate margin \|head − plan rate\| × 20 ms × range | 15 | 55.3% → 55.4%, median miss 5.6 → 5.9 cm | 92.7% → 93.4% | 85.7% → 91.4%, 2.66 → 3.07 hits/s |

None reduced drive miss distance. The gate margin helps spin under detector
noise. It changes firing on the robot, so it needs its own review before
shipping (`fw-slew-margin.patch` in the evidence).

## Open

- Drive accuracy needs the head to track a moving plan, or a gate that
  rejects edge shots without losing drive hits/s (a fixed inset lost them in
  the [shot-accuracy study](sentry-shot-accuracy-2026-11-26.md#result)).
  The tracker's cross-line-of-sight velocity bias while driving is the next
  tracker item.
- Run verdicts still depend on load, though shot outcomes do not. These
  rejected runs with unchanged shots:
  - `gz_bridge`'s 3 s teardown watchdog;
  - `lidar_self_filter` ignoring SIGINT for 15 s;
  - an `e2e_suite` exit hang;
  - bring-up timeouts: a `nav_goal` ack at 0.1 s, and gz not held at
    0.015 s.
