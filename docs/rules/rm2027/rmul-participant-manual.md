## 1 Preface

The RoboMaster University Series (RMU), initiated by DJI, is one of the events under the National College Student Robot Competition and is a robot competition and academic exchange platform created for technology enthusiasts worldwide. Since its founding in 2013, it has always upheld the philosophy of "giving glory to youth, giving thinking its power, and serving young engineers worldwide in becoming dreamers who pursue excellence and are action-oriented", and is committed to cultivating and attracting well-rounded talents with an engineering mindset, while spreading the beauty of technology and the idea of technological innovation widely to the public.

The National College Student Robot Competition RoboMaster University Championship (RMUC) focuses on examining participants' comprehensive application of science and engineering disciplines and their engineering practice ability. It fully integrates numerous robot-related technical disciplines such as "mechanical engineering", "embedded system design", "control algorithms", "electrical engineering", "integrated communication and sensing", "autonomous navigation" and "human-machine interaction". It also innovatively combines popular presentation styles with robot competition, making robot confrontation more intuitive and intense, and has attracted the wide attention of many technology enthusiasts and the general public.

The National College Student Robot Competition RoboMaster University League (RMUL) is bid for by local academic institutions and universities and draws in surrounding universities to participate. It aims to promote regional exchange of university robot technology, foster a strong academic atmosphere, and support regional scientific and technological innovation and development.

RMUL has two challenge events: the 3V3 Match and the Infantry Match.

## 2 Participation

### 2.1 Registration Requirements

3V3 Match: no registration restrictions

Infantry Match: teams that successfully took part in an RMUC 2025 or RMUC 2026 offline competition must not register for the RMUL 2027 Infantry Match

### 2.2 Participating Teams

> Note:
> - Participating teams are divided into Tier-A teams and non-Tier-A teams. For details, see the "Points System".
> - Due to restrictions of overseas laws and regulations, teams from overseas regions and countries cannot yet create teams or register on the official RoboMaster platform. Team creation and registration, the subsequent technical assessment stages and personnel list collection for overseas teams will all be conducted through Wenjuanxing. Overseas team registration entry: https://djistore.wjx.cn/vm/Q4bFVhT.aspx

Participating teams must follow these principles:

**R1** All participating teams must have the team's person in charge (team captain or team leader teacher) create the team on the RoboMaster Forum; teams that already exist do not need to be created again. When creating a team, team information must be completed as the team requires, and the invitation code must be obtained by contacting RoboMaster competition affairs staff. After the team is ready, the team's person in charge can invite participating team members to join through an invitation link. For the specific registration process, see the Registration Guide. Registration succeeds if all requirements of this chapter are met. The Organizing Committee will open the participant list adjustment function from time to time during the season, and teams may change their participating members during the open period. All information is based on what is confirmed by the last click on "confirm list" within the current open period; it cannot be changed after the open period is missed. Before each event is held, the registration center will open one last time, and the Organizing Committee will use the finally submitted information to produce and issue award certificates.

**R2** The same participating team must use the same team name in different events. The team name of a participating team must take the form "XXX Team", where "XXX" is a name customized by the team. The customized name must not exceed 16 characters (each Chinese character counts as 2 characters, each English letter counts as 1 character), and must not contain the school name, an abbreviation of the school name, an English abbreviation of the school name, words such as "队", "团队" or "战队" (all meaning "team"), or special symbols such as "*/-+". The team name must reflect the team's enterprising spirit and comply with relevant national laws and regulations. If the Organizing Committee determines that a team name does not fit the spirit of the competition, it has the right to require the team to revise its name.

> Note: After a participating team submits its registration materials, the team name may not be changed.

**R3** A participating team must be based at one institution of higher education, and must meet the requirements for personnel roles, numbers and status specified in "2.3 Participants".

**R4** In principle, in the same event (challenge event), only one participating team from a school is eligible to participate. If a school's campuses are not in the same city or a similar situation makes it impossible for students who intend to participate to form a team together, the school is allowed to form teams from different campuses after verification by the Organizing Committee. If a school has more than one team registering, the specific registration review rules are given in

<!-- p.7 -->
the "RoboMaster 2027 University Series Representative Team List Announcement". Registrants must ensure that registration information is complete and accurate and bear the corresponding responsibility; registrants bear all consequences of omissions or errors in registration information. In special circumstances, they may contact the Organizing Committee to explain, and the Organizing Committee will handle the matter according to the situation. The Organizing Committee reserves the right of final interpretation.

> Note: If a participating team fails to meet any one of R1-R4, the Organizing Committee will reject the registration application. The team may resubmit after revising it to meet the requirements.

**R5** Joint teams are not allowed.

> Note: A team that registered for RMUC as a joint team in the current season is not allowed to register for RMUL under the same joint team name.

**R6** Any participant is allowed to take part in only one participating team throughout this season.

**R7** If participating teams fail to satisfy any one of the "Five Differences" principle, they are regarded as the same participating team.

> Note:
> - The "Five Differences" principle: different team names, different team members, different advisor teachers, different affiliated organizations (school units such as colleges), different participating robots.
> - In RMUL, the 3V3 Match and the Infantry Match are regarded as a whole, that is, different teams from the same school are not allowed to take part separately in the 3V3 Match and the Infantry Match.
> - The first principle for distinguishing different participating teams of the same school is the team name.

**R8** Provided that the registration requirements of the relevant events are met, one participating team may register for and take part in different events of the RoboMaster University Series at the same time (including RMUC and RMUL).

**R9** The Organizing Committee regards the same participating team taking part in different events in the same season as a whole and handles all event affairs processes uniformly (including material donation, material purchase, participation support, etc.). After season registration ends, the whole cannot be split.

All participating teams must complete the corresponding Technical Assessment stages within the specified time ranges and according to RMOC requirements, taking the initiative to fully demonstrate their team's strength.

> Note:
> - The number of First and Second Prizes is calculated as the number of teams in each group that advance to the knockout stage * the award ratio.
> - If placements cannot be determined after grouping, teams are ranked by time-averaged net control advantage.

### Appendix Participation Safety Notice

All RoboMaster participants must fully understand that safety is the most important prerequisite for the continued development of the competition. To protect the rights and interests of all participants and the event organizers, and in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, by registering for RoboMaster, all participants acknowledge and agree to abide by the following safety terms:

1. All participants must guarantee that they have full capacity for civil conduct and the ability to independently build and operate robots, and guarantee that before building robots with products of the event organizer, SZ DJI Technology Co., Ltd., they will carefully read the registration notice, the competition rules and other relevant documents.
2. During the event, guarantee that the building, testing and use of all robots will not cause harm to their own team members or opposing team members, referees, staff, spectators, equipment or the competition venue.
3. Guarantee that the robot structure design takes into account the convenience of the robot safety inspection during Pre-Match Inspection, and actively cooperate with the organizer's Pre-Match Inspection.
4. The use of fuel-powered engines, explosives and hazardous chemical materials is prohibited, as is the use of hydraulic or other power methods that may cause pollution.
5. At any stage of R&D, preparation and participation, team members must pay full attention to safety and comply with the *RoboMaster University Series Battery Safety Specifications*; advisors should take responsibility for safety guidance and supervision.
6. Guarantee the safety of robots and ensure that their launching mechanisms are in a safe state, so that at no time will they directly or indirectly injure team members, referees, staff or spectators.
7. During R&D, training and competition, take sufficient and necessary safety measures against possible accidents, for example: wear safety goggles; avoid loss of control of the control system; urge team members to think through operating steps beforehand to avoid misoperation and collisions between team members and between team members and robots; strictly prohibit team members from training alone, to ensure someone can respond to accidents in an emergency; during debugging, appropriate safety measures such as proper locking and emergency stop switches must be added to the robot system.
8. All accident liability and corresponding losses arising in practice and competition from unexpected situations such as robot failure or loss of control of an unmanned aerial vehicle in flight are borne by the participating team that caused the accident.
9. All participating teams must strictly comply with local laws and regulations on flight control. A team's Aerial Robot flying above the competition field in a specific restricted area of the venue is indoor flight and does not involve airspace approval. To ensure the flight safety of the Aerial Robot, it is connected to a ground anchor post by a rope; if an accident such as the Aerial Robot breaking free of the safety tether occurs, the pilot must land and stop the propellers as soon as possible, and continued flight is strictly prohibited. Flying a team's Aerial Robot outdoors is strictly prohibited.
10. Items sold and provided by the event organizer, SZ DJI Technology Co., Ltd., such as batteries and the Referee System, must be used according to the instruction documents. If anyone is injured through improper use, SZ DJI Technology Co., Ltd. bears no liability. Personal injury and property loss to oneself or any third party caused by building or operating robots are borne by the participating team itself.
